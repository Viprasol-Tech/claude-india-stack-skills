# Getting help

1. **Read the [README](README.md)** and the skill's `SKILL.md` and `reference.md`.
2. **Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Viprasol-Tech/claude-india-stack-skills/discussions)**
   for how-to questions and to share what you built.
3. **Open an [issue](https://github.com/Viprasol-Tech/claude-india-stack-skills/issues)** for a bug
   or a wrong fact, with the skill name and what you expected.
4. **Need a UPI, GST, KYC or fintech product built for India?** Viprasol Tech does this
   professionally: [viprasol.com](https://viprasol.com) · [support@viprasol.com](mailto:support@viprasol.com).
